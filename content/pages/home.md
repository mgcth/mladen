Hi!

I'm a Senior ML Engineer at eghed, currently working with Trafikverket, where I initiated and now lead the development of vision foundation models for railway tracks.
I've used LeJEPA to train a ViT on over 500 TB of railway images and deployed a model classifying Sweden's complete population of over 20 million sleepers multiple times a year.

Previously, I was a Senior Data Scientist at Volvo Cars where I developed a deep-learning posture detection system based on 3D cameras used for internal <a href="https://research.chalmers.se/publication/538201" target="_blank">research</a> and <a href="https://worldwide.espacenet.com/patent/search/family/087158014/publication/EP4488961A1?q=pn%3DEP4488961A1" target="_blank">patented</a> a method for detecting missing head keypoints.

My background is in Dynamics; specifically, I have a
<a href="https://research.chalmers.se/en/publication/514656" target="_blank">PhD in identification of dynamical systems</a>.
During that time, I was awarded <a href="https://sem.org/awardsharting" target="_blank">SEM's Harting Award</a> for research on <a href="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40799-019-00317-z" target="_blank">state-space based dynamic substructuring</a>.

<a href="https://mladen.gibanica.net/projects/">projects</a> and other interests.
